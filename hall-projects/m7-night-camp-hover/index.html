Night Camp ranger school

We invite you to learn how to survive 99 nights in the forest. We teach fire building, tent making, map reading and night watch. Our classes are taught by professional rangers in small groups of different levels, from first-night beginners to rangers who have lasted all 99 nights.

Fire building Tent making Night watch

About us

Night Camp opened on the first night in the forest. Since then we have taught hundreds of survivors how to stay safe until morning. Our rangers know every path, every river and every strange sound in the dark. Come and learn from the people who have seen it all.

What to bring

  • An axe for wood
  • A flashlight
  • Bandages
  • Warm clothes
  • A bottle of water

Our classes
